Keep the house money safe

A bank RD is intended for capital preservation, not high returns. DICGC deposit insurance is generally capped at ₹5 lakh per depositor per bank, including eligible principal and interest across deposits at that bank. Consider spreading the ₹75,000 monthly deposits across multiple insured banks, and account for any existing deposits at each bank. Compare actual 18-month RD rates, early-closure terms and tax treatment before opening accounts.
